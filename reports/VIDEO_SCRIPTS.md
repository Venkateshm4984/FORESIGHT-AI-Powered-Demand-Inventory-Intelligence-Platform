# Demo Video Script (3–4 minutes)

1. Introduce yourself and the project: “I’m Venkatesh M. This is FORESIGHT, an AI-powered demand forecasting and inventory intelligence dashboard.”
2. Open the Overview page. Explain the historical units sold, current inventory value, SKUs needing action, and forecast demand.
3. Point out the historical sales trend and inventory-health chart.
4. Open Demand Forecast. Select a SKU and explain the actual-versus-forecast chart and the forecast horizon control.
5. Open Inventory Recommendations. Explain stockout risk, reorder soon, reorder point, suggested order quantity, and CSV export.
6. Open Data Explorer. Show the dataset and explain that users can upload a CSV with the required fields.
7. Close with limitations: sample data is synthetic; real deployment should use real authorized data and validate forecast accuracy.

# Feedback / Reflection Video Script (1–2 minutes)

- What I learned: data preprocessing, feature engineering, forecasting, dashboard design, and translating predictions into inventory decisions.
- Challenge: balancing a useful forecasting prototype with the limited history and quality of sample data.
- What I would improve: time-based backtesting, forecast accuracy metrics, promotion/holiday features, and integration with inventory systems.
- Final takeaway: the project turns sales history into a clear, explainable planning workflow.
